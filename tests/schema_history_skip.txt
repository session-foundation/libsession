# reviewable change rather than a silent one.

# The hashes must be those of the commits as merged.  A branch rebased after its entries were
# written leaves them naming commits that are not in the history at all -- which skips nothing, and
# which the walk refuses rather than quietly checking everything they were meant to cover.
#
# Format:  <short-hash>  <reason>

# and cannot take the replacement, or the replacement recorded without what was added to it later.
